CogAbility · Issue #277
1 · What was wrong

Callers with no topic went to the wrong queue

A caller who just asked for a person ("representative", "customer service") landed at Property Tax instead of DMV / Driver License.

2 · Why it happened

The Router let a wrong guess through

DMV / Driver License was already the default. When a caller gave no topic, CogBot sometimes guessed Property Tax, and the Router only corrected picks of DMV, so the guess went through.

3 · The fix

No topic: Router resets to DMV

Caller just asks for a person. If CogBot picks Property Tax but no Property Tax words were said, the Router sends the call to DMV / Driver License, the default.

4 · The fix

Topic said earlier: Router keeps it

Caller wants to talk about their property tax. If they said "property tax" within the last several turns, the Router sends them to Property Tax, even if CogBot picked DMV when they later just say "get me a human".

5 · The fix

Back taxes: Delinquent Tax

Caller asks about a tax certificate / back taxes. (Illustrative — no real Delinquent Tax transfers in the latest window.)
