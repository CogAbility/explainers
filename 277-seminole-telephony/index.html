CogAbility · Issue #277
1 · The report

Seminole County told us some callers reached Property Tax instead of DMV / Driver License.

2 · Why it happened

A no-topic call was sent to Property Tax.

The caller named no topic, so it belonged at DMV. The transfer request was filled in as Property Tax anyway, and Detect caller’s topic was skipped.

3 · The fix

Now the Detect caller’s topic step runs on every transfer.

Detect caller’s topic now checks every transfer request. No topic found → DMV.
